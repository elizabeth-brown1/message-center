# Average Learning Coach Experience Analysis

**Population analyzed:** 3,646 unique students (proxy for 3,646 Learning Coach experiences)  
**Total interactions:** 100,019 logged records  
**School year coverage:** July 2025 – May 2026

Rather than focusing on intervention operations overall, this analysis answers:

> *"What does the average Learning Coach (LC) experience during a school year?"*

***

# Executive Summary

The average Learning Coach receives approximately:

* **27.4 total communications per school year**
* **2.8 communications per month**
* **0.65 communications per week**
* **0.09 communications per day**

In practice:

> The typical Learning Coach receives fewer than one communication per week, but that communication is most likely related to parent outreach, attendance, engagement, academic progress, graduation planning, or school-required notifications.

***

# Top 3 Insights

***

## Insight 1: The Average LC Experience Is Dominated by Direct Parent Outreach

### Average Annual Messages per Learning Coach

| Log Type         | Avg. Messages per LC/Year |
| ---------------- | ------------------------: |
| Parent Contact   |                 **12.75** |
| Stride Email     |                  **6.96** |
| Student Contact  |                  **3.85** |
| Office Messages  |                  **1.72** |
| Special Programs |                  **1.64** |
| All Others       |                      <0.5 |

### What This Means

Nearly half of all communications experienced by an average Learning Coach come through Parent Contact records.

### Executive Translation

> The Learning Coach experience is heavily relationship-centered rather than administration-centered. Families primarily interact with the school through personalized outreach rather than automated systems.

***

### Strategic Recommendation

Develop a dedicated Family Engagement model that treats Learning Coaches as a core stakeholder group rather than a secondary audience.

***

## Insight 2: "No Connection" Is One of the Most Common Experiences

### Average Annual Subtype Experience

| Log Subtype                 | Avg. Messages per LC/Year |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------: |
| No Connection               |                  **4.48** |
| Made Connection             |                  **3.36** |
| Connection Attempt          |                  **0.91** |
| Grad Plan Fall Conference   |                  **0.22** |
| Grad Plan Spring Conference |                  **0.13** |

### Key Finding

The average Learning Coach experiences:

* More documented outreach failures than successful connections.

For every typical Learning Coach:

* \~4.5 No Connection attempts
* \~3.4 successful connections

occur during the year.

### Executive Translation

> Communication friction is embedded in the average family experience. Families are contacted frequently, but successful engagement does not always occur.

***

### Strategic Recommendation

Introduce communication preference profiling:

* Text-first families
* Email-first families
* Phone-first families

Reducing failed contacts would likely improve both family satisfaction and student outcomes.

***

## Insight 3: The Average Family Experience Is Highly Seasonal

### Communication Peaks

Highest-volume months:

| Month   | Volume |
| ------- | -----: |
| January | 14,487 |
| May     | 13,461 |
| March   | 11,549 |

### Why These Months Matter

January:

* Semester start
* Testing
* Report cards
* Graduation planning

March:

* Progress reporting
* Graduation audits
* Re-registration

May:

* Graduation
* Credit recovery
* Final grades
* Summer school

### Executive Translation

> Families do not receive a steady stream of communication. The majority of their interactions occur during specific academic pressure periods.

***

### Strategic Recommendation

Create a monthly communication rhythm that smooths engagement across the year instead of concentrating communication near deadlines.

# How Representative Is the Average Experience?

Using all 3,646 students represented in the dataset:

### Average Annual Touchpoints

27.4 messages

This figure is highly representative because:

* The dataset spans an entire academic year.
* It contains more than 100,000 logged interactions.
* It includes students from both AZVA and ISAZ.
* It captures regular education, graduation support, MTSS interventions, Special Programs, and re-registration.

### Practical Interpretation

Most families likely fall within:

**20–35 communications annually**

with outliers experiencing:

* Significantly more messages if on graduation watch lists
* Significantly fewer messages if highly independent and academically successful

***

# Novel Insight

## Learning Coaches Experience the School Primarily During Moments of Risk

The average family's interaction pattern is not:

> "Here's how your child is succeeding."

It is more often:

> "Here's something that requires your attention."

Evidence:

The dominant high-volume subtypes include:

* No Connection
* Made Connection
* Connection Attempt
* Progress concerns
* Attendance concerns
* Graduation risk
* Credit recovery

Recognition, celebration, and positive outreach represent a much smaller percentage of communications.

### Executive Translation

> From the Learning Coach perspective, the school often appears most visible when there is a problem to solve. This suggests an opportunity to increase proactive positive-touch communications to improve overall family engagement and school perception.

***

# Bottom Line

The **average Learning Coach experience** can be summarized as:

> **27 communications annually, mostly centered around parent outreach, student progress, and intervention-related support, with communication activity heavily concentrated around major academic milestones such as semester starts, progress-report periods, graduation planning, and credit-recovery deadlines.**

The most powerful improvement opportunity is likely not increasing communication volume—but increasing the proportion of communications that result in successful engagement and positive outcomes.
